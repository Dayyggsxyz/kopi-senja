02

Six seats, one grinder, and beans roasted a few streets away.

We opened Kopi Senja in 2019 with six seats and a single hand grinder. Batu turns cold most evenings, so the lamps stay low, the water stays hot, and nobody will rush you out the door.

Beans come from small farms in Gayo, Ijen, and Kintamani, roasted in town every Tuesday. Milk comes from a dairy outside Malang. The pisang goreng is fried when you order it, which is why it takes ten minutes.
